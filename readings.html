Receive a Reading

Psychic work rooted in embodiment, spiritual clarity, and the wisdom of the soul.

While many people associate the word psychic with predicting the future, the interpretation here is quite different.

The etymology of the word psychic means “of the soul.” A reading is not about handing someone answers or creating dependency. Rather, it is a process of helping someone recognize and reconnect with their own unique spiritual frequency.

Through receiving a reading, many people experience a deeper sense of embodiment, greater spiritual clarity, emotional insight, increased ease in life, and a renewed relationship with their own intuition.

The purpose of this work is not to tell you who you are, but to help illuminate what already exists within you.

Students who commit to ongoing study through the Institute for Psychic Herbalism are given opportunities to practice and refine their reading skills in a grounded, supportive environment.
