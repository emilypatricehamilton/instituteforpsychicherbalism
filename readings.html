Receive a Reading

Psychic work rooted in embodiment, spiritual clarity, and the wisdom of the soul.

While many people associate the word psychic with predicting the future, the interpretation here is quite different.

The etymology of the word psychic means “of the soul.” A reading is not about handing someone answers or creating dependency. Rather, it is a process of helping someone recognize and reconnect with their own unique spiritual frequency.

Through receiving a reading, many people experience a deeper sense of embodiment, greater spiritual clarity, emotional insight, increased ease in life, and a renewed relationship with their own intuition.

The purpose of this work is not to tell you who you are, but to help illuminate what already exists within you.

Students who commit to ongoing study through the Institute for Psychic Herbalism are given opportunities to practice and refine their reading skills in a grounded, supportive environment.

Professional Readings & Spiritual Counseling

Emily is a licensed spiritual counselor with over ten years of clairvoyant training, reading, and teaching experience. Her work is devoted to helping individuals reconnect with their innate spiritual frequency in areas of life that may feel heavy, uncertain, painful, or confused.

Rather than offering fixed answers or predictions, these sessions are designed to create greater energetic clarity, release stagnant emotional patterns, and restore ease and movement within the body and spirit.

Herbal Expansion Readings begin through attunement to your unique soul frequency and allowing that expansion to settle into the body. From this space, we move gently into the questions or areas of life seeking clarity, healing, or transformation.

The purpose of the reading is not to tell you what to do, but to help you become more deeply connected to your own inner knowing. Through this process, individuals often experience increased intuitive clarity, emotional release, embodiment, and spiritual insight.

During the session, plant and fungal allies may also arise in support of the work. Emily shares intuitive communication surrounding these relationships and the energetic medicine they may carry for you at this time.
