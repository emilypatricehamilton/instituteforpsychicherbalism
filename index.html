Institute for Psychic Herbalism

A space for spiritual relationship with the intelligence of plant medicine.

The Institute for Psychic Herbalism is devoted to the awakening of our innate spiritual wisdom and intuitive relationship with the natural world. While many systems of herbal education focus primarily on memorization and rote knowledge, this work invites students into direct experience.

Beyond learning what a plant is used for, we ask deeper questions: Where does this wisdom originate? What intelligence lives within the plant itself? What message, medicine, or transformation is seeking relationship with you specifically?

Through a precise, easeful, and repeatable method for spiritually communing with plant medicine, students learn to perceive the energetic and psychic dimensions of herbalism firsthand. These practices are paired with tools for energetic healing, intuitive development, embodiment, and deepening relationship with the living world.

The Institute for Psychic Herbalism exists as both a school and a sacred space — one devoted to healing, perception, spiritual remembrance, and the rediscovery of nature as an intelligent and living presence.
