Psychic Herbalism

A 12-week immersion into energetic healing, spiritual perception, and plant consciousness.

The Psychic Herbalism program begins with the cultivation of a deep embodied relationship with one’s own energy. Students are guided through the creation of a series of energetic healing tools and practices designed to restore clarity, strengthen clairvoyance, and transform the energy body.

As the program unfolds, we begin spiritually connecting with the consciousness and medicine of specific plants and mushrooms. While attuned to the energetic frequency of each medicine, students learn to utilize these tools for healing, intuitive development, insight, and spiritual transformation.

No physical plants are required for this work. The connection is entirely energetic — an experience that reveals the profound relationship between spirit, consciousness, and the living intelligence of the natural world.

Through direct experience, students awaken a deeper trust in their innate sensitivity, intuition, and capacity for spiritual perception.

Program Journey
Weeks 1–3 · Building the Energetic Foundation

In the first three weeks of the program, we focus on developing a foundational energetic toolkit. Through direct experience, students learn a host of energetic healing tools while discovering how each practice uniquely works for them.

This phase of the program is devoted to embodiment, grounding, energetic awareness, and creating safety and clarity within the energy body before moving into spiritual connection with plant medicine.

Weeks 4–8 · Vibrational Plant & Mushroom Medicine

In the following five weeks, students begin spiritually connecting with plants and mushrooms that support various body systems including the skin, nervous system, heart, muscle and skeletal systems, and immune system.

Each medicine carries its own energetic signature and frequency. Through these experiences, students begin developing a unique “vibrational medicine kit” while deepening their intuitive relationship with the consciousness of each plant ally.

Weeks 9–12 · Clairvoyance Through Time & Space

In the final four weeks, we begin exploring how different medicines have been utilized throughout time. This portion of the program focuses deeply on the development of clairvoyance and the ability to perceive energy across time and space.

This stage of the work often surprises students. It can feel like opening a gift that has quietly been waiting within them all along. Many students who previously doubted their clairvoyant ability are amazed by both the clarity of what they perceive and how natural the experience feels.
